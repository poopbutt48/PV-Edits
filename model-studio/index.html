    2 · Supports & loads

      A patch is the part of the clicked face within the radius. Supports fix every surface node in it; loads are split evenly over its nodes.

      3 · Static analysis

      4 · Topology optimization

      Method: 3D SIMP (penalty 3) with a density filter and optimality-criteria updates, minimising compliance at the volume you set. Each design iteration solves a full linear-elastic FE model on the voxel grid (8-node hex elements).
